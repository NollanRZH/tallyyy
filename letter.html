A Letter for You

This 31st day of May, 2026 · Somewhere upon this world
My love, my mommy, my master, my princess Taleen

Where do I even begin with you? How do I find the words to tell you, to truly remind you, just how deeply and completely I love you? I have written you long good morning messages, long good night messages, words that probably overflowed your screen and still felt too short for everything I wanted to say. And every single time, I meant every letter of every word.

You have this power over me, Taleen, and I do not say that lightly. I am yours in every sense of the word. You can give me orders, and I will follow them gladly, not because I have to but because making you happy is something that genuinely makes me feel whole. Treating you like the queen, the princess that you are, never feels like an effort. It feels like the most natural thing in the world.

I wait for your notifications. Sometimes I jump on them the second they arrive. And sometimes, when something hurt me or unsettled me, I take a breath first. I step back. Because I refuse to say something careless, something that could wound you even slightly. I think before I act when it comes to you, always, because you matter far too much for anything less.

And yes, I will be honest with you: it stings a little when I reply to you instantly, heart wide open, and then the silence stretches for twenty, thirty, forty minutes before you come back. Then it starts again. I am not angry about it. I just love you so much that every quiet minute without you feels longer than it should.

I stay up sometimes, just to receive that one good night message from you. My favourite one. The one that makes everything quiet and right in my chest. I have you as my wallpaper. I have you in my bio, and you should know, I never wanted a bio. I never cared for one. Until you. That means something. That means everything.

I told you once that I love you the way I love my club. I was born in Rennes, I grew up with that crest in my chest, with the ultras, with the loyalty and the fire and the belonging that comes with it. Being an ultra teaches you something: real loyalty is not just there when things are easy. It stays when it is hard, when it hurts, when the stands are cold and the score is bad. You break your voice, not hearts. You stand for your people through everything. That is how I love you, Taleen. That is the weight of it.

So this letter is not a grand declaration. It is simply a reminder, written by candlelight, sealed with everything I have, that you are thought of, waited for, chosen every single day. My love for you is not a fleeting thing. It is loyal. It is loud. And it is entirely, irreversibly yours.

Entirely and forever yours,

your good boy
